Human Resources Policies

Vacation and Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year in their first three years of employment. After three years, accrual increases to 20 days per year. Vacation days do not roll over at the end of the calendar year; unused days are forfeited. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 days into the new year with manager approval.

Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick days do not accrue and reset on January 1st each year. If an illness extends beyond 5 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Extended illness beyond 30 days may qualify for short-term disability coverage.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work arrangements require a stable internet connection and a dedicated workspace free from significant distractions. Employees working remotely are expected to be available during core hours of 10am-3pm in their local time zone. Full-remote arrangements for specific roles must be approved by HR and the department head.

Onboarding Process

New employees complete a structured onboarding program during their first 30 days. This includes IT setup (Day 1), HR paperwork and benefits enrollment (Days 1-3), team introductions and role orientation (Week 1), and a 30-day check-in with their manager. Employees receive access to the internal knowledge base, communication tools, and development environments on Day 1.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews occur twice per year: mid-year in June and year-end in December. The review process includes self-assessment, peer feedback (for senior roles), and manager evaluation. Compensation adjustments resulting from reviews take effect on January 1st and July 1st respectively. Employees on a performance improvement plan (PIP) may have modified review schedules.

Benefits and Compensation

The company offers health, dental, and vision insurance with the company covering 80% of premiums for employees and 50% for dependents. A 401(k) plan with 4% company match is available after 90 days of employment. Additional benefits include a $1,500 annual professional development stipend, a $100/month wellness allowance, and 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all parents.

Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, treat colleagues with respect, and uphold the company's values of integrity, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination. Concerns should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.
